# 苏轼《水调歌头·明月几时有》土家语翻译（许渊冲英译直译版）

**作者**：宋·苏轼  
**版本说明**：本版以**许渊冲英译本为源文本**，逐行直译成土家语（而非从中文原词出发）。许渊冲英译注重押韵与意译，其行文本身已是"再创作"，本版忠实还原英译的意象取舍与句法结构。与前两版（中文直译版 `水调歌头_土家语翻译.md`、许渊冲意译版 `水调歌头_土家语翻译_许渊冲版.md`）形成三版对照。

**许渊冲英译原文（本文所据底本）**：

```
How long will the full moon appear?
Wine cup in hand, I ask the sky.
I do not know what time of year
It would be tonight in the palace on high.
Riding the wind, there I would fly,
Yet I'm afraid the crystalline palace would be
Too high and cold for me.
I rise and dance, with my shadow I play.
On high as on earth, would it be as gay?

The moon goes round the mansions red
Through gauze-draped windows to shed
Her light upon the sleepless bed.
Against man she ought to have no spite.
Why then when people part, is she oft full and bright?
Men have sorrow and joy, they meet or part again;
The moon is bright or dim and she may wax or wane.
There has been nothing perfect since the olden days.
So let us wish that man
May live long as he can!
Though miles apart, we'll share the beauty she displays.
```

> 转写依据：`docs/comp.md`（叶德书1984拼音方案）+ `docs/拼音系统.md`。声调尾字母 55→x、53→v、35→f、21→r；韵母 ɤ→e、ɿ→ii、iɛ→ie、鼻化 ã→an/ũ→un；声母 x→h、k→g、ʦ→z、ʨʰ→q、p→b、t→d、ɣ→hx（B词典 正字）。
